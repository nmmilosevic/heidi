An atelier of sensory design.

Heidi Gubbins Interior Design is an award-winning boutique atelier with a curated approach to every facet of the home — always favouring fresh ideas and inspired solutions over ‘off the shelf’.

The studio, Golden Mile — MarbellaEst. Marbella
I use the light, dimensions, materials and architectural details as my ingredients.Heidi Gubbins

Working on an international scale from Marbella, the studio specialises in residential and commercial turnkey projects — beautifully styled, balanced and proportioned spaces.

Each design begins with the architecture of the property and builds upon it to create a strong sense of identity: unmistakably signed, yet as unique as the project itself.

01

Residential Design

Homes designed around the personal tastes and lifestyles of those who live in them, elevated by the spatial insight of a skilled professional — and an element of unexpected enchantment.

Collections are personally sourced, varied and made in small numbers. Trends never dominate; timeless appeal and individual expression do.

“It’s a journey… a creative experience where passion and professionalism are in balance.”

02

Commercial Projects

Creative concept paired with rigour on deadlines, budgets and quality — supported by stylists, carpenters, architects, landscapers, lighting specialists and technicians.

Bars, restaurants, beach clubs, offices, retail and boutique hotels: larger scale, commercial realities, and the same insistence on getting it right.

“The two building blocks of a successful project are the strength of the concept and the quality of its execution in detail.”

03

Refurbishment & Renovations

From a single room to the full refurbishment of a large property, the atelier’s technical and design teams build on a home’s architecture to make it look its very best again.

A rich textural and tonal palette of natural materials creates a dialogue between spaces, forms and individual pieces.

“I am passionate about creating a mesmerising interplay between materials, textures, tones, shapes and dimensions.”

04

Bespoke Furniture Design

One-of-a-kind and limited-edition pieces, produced to measure by skilled craftsmen. Artists are sought out — and commissioned — for work that creates impact and elicits emotion.

Every piece carries the DNA of the space and of those who live in it.

“There is no standard style — that is the beauty.”

The process

Listen

Concept

Craft

Deliver

Turnkey execution with rigour on deadlines, budget and quality.
