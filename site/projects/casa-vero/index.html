Casa Vero

Project
Casa Vero
Location
Marbella
Typology
Private Residence
Category
Residential

A home shaped around an effervescent personality — whimsical and artistic, yet inherently calm.

Casa Vero was conceived as a portrait of its owner: her warmth, her strength and her love of family and entertaining. A quiet, neutral foundation lets bold artistic gestures take the lead.

Large-scale murals and curated art borrow their hues from nature and the slow tones of sunset. In the cinema room a textured dark leather wall sets an immersive mood, arching murals extend the story, and playful upholstery keeps sophistication and joy in balance.
